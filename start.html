The Things We Were Never Told

Welcome to an interactive experience inspired by A Wrinkle in Time. Step into Meg’s world and experience her story as you help her find her missing father and uncover more along the way.

Characters

Meg Murry

A curious and determined teenager trying to find her missing father. As she uncovers information that has been erased, she begins questioning what she has been taught to trust.

Mr Murry

Meg's father, who disappeared while working on a secret government technology project. His research holds clues to information hidden from the public.

Charles Wallace

Meg's younger brother. Intelligent and observant, he begins trusting the platform's version of the truth, creating distance between himself and his family.

The Three W's

Ma Whatsit, Ma Who and Ma Which are mysterious guides who help Meg question what she sees online and uncover knowledge that IT has tried to erase.
