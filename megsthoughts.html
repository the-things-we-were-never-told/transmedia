MEG'S THOUGHTS

What I found

I think I'm starting to understand what Dad was trying to show me. MzanziConnect might be more than just a social media platform. Everything I've found on the Drive has been removed or hidden by IT. What if IT isn't only controlling what people see, but also what they remember? Languages, songs, stories, photographs and knowledge that have been passed down for generations.

It’s almost like they're not just removing information, but slowly removing parts of people's history and identity too. They make one way of understanding the world seem more important than everything else. If people can't find or remember these things anymore, eventually they might stop seeing them as important.

Dad must have realised this and created the Drive. He tried to expose IT, and they took him before he could. But I think Dad knew there was more to knowledge than what IT wanted people to believe. It seems like the Mrs. W's have been protecting the things IT tried to erase in my father's absence.

I'm starting to understand why Charles has been changing too. He seems more willing to accept what IT tells him, and I wonder if it's because he hasn't been given a reason to question it. Maybe that's why he's been pulling away from the things our family has always valued. I'm worried that the more he trusts IT, the harder it'll become for him to recognise the parts of himself that don't fit its version of the world. I still want to find Dad, but I think I'm starting to understand that this is bigger than him. I need to save Charles too.

I've always felt like I don't quite fit in, and maybe I've been looking at that the wrong way. Maybe part of it comes from not fully understanding my own history and where I come from. The more I find, the more I wonder how much of that has been hidden from me too.

I don't know if I'm right yet. But if I am, then finding Dad might be about more than bringing him home. Maybe it's also about finding out who we are before someone else decides that for us.
