What We Choose to Remember

Meg helped her dad escape IT by sending him the old archives he had worked so hard to protect. As he watched the memories he had been forced to forget, he began to remember who he was. The archive broke IT's hold over him and helped him find his way back to his family.

Meg understood that the archive was about more than recovering information. It was about protecting their heritage, their languages and the stories passed down through families and communities. These were the things that connected people to where they came from, and IT had no right to decide that they were worth forgetting.

She planned to show Charles the archive too. IT had made him believe that its version of the world was the only one that mattered, but Meg hoped that seeing the truth would help him question what he had been taught. She wanted him to understand that knowing your history and listening to your community could help you think for yourself. She wasn't going to give up on her brother.

Ma Whatsit, Ma Who and Ma Which had helped Meg find her way when she felt completely lost. Once their part was done, the three of them disappeared as mysteriously as they had arrived.

There was still work to do. More stories needed to be recovered, more people needed to remember, and more of the truth needed to be shared. But Meg had her dad back, and she knew she could help Charles find his way back to himself too. She had learned that heritage wasn't just something you inherited but also something you protected, shared and kept alive together.

IT could hide their stories, but it couldn't erase who they were. As long as people remembered their roots and stood together, their history would never truly be lost.
